About me

My professional work focuses on developing maintainable backend systems, integrating services through APIs, improving application workflows, and coordinating delivery with clients, developers, and QA teams. At JBRSOFT Limited, I have contributed to national and enterprise-level digital platforms, including public-health and regulatory systems.

I completed an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Jahangirnagar University. My research examined machine-learning approaches to software project delay-risk prediction, connecting practical software engineering concerns with predictive analytics and experimental evaluation.

I am interested in the intersection of software engineering, applied machine learning, software quality, and evidence-informed decision support.
