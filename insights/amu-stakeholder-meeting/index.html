PROJECT UPDATE · 2025

Stakeholder Engagement for AMU Surveillance

Stakeholder engagement is an important part of developing a national digital surveillance platform. Discussions around the National AMU Surveillance System brought together relevant partners to support a coordinated approach to antimicrobial-use monitoring.

The project involves collaboration between DGDA, WHO Bangladesh, and JBRSOFT Limited, with the broader aim of strengthening evidence-informed AMU surveillance in Bangladesh.
