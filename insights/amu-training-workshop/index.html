Training on the National AMU Surveillance System

A three-day hands-on technical training organized by WHO Bangladesh, JBRSOFT, and DGDA.

I served as a trainer for a three-day hands-on technical training on the National AMU Surveillance System of Bangladesh. The sessions supported government officials in understanding AMU data management, validation, and reporting workflows.

The experience highlighted the importance of practical training and continued user support when introducing digital tools for public-health monitoring.
