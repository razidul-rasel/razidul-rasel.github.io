PROJECT UPDATE · 2025

Inception Meeting: National AMU Surveillance System

The inception meeting marked the beginning of collaborative work on the National AMU Surveillance System for DGDA Bangladesh, with WHO Bangladesh and JBRSOFT Limited as partners.

The initiative focuses on strengthening antimicrobial-use surveillance through digital data collection and reporting workflows, supporting a coordinated national approach.
