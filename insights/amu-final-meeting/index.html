PROJECT UPDATE · 2025

Final Stakeholder Meeting for the National AMU System

The National AMU Surveillance System is a collaborative digital health initiative involving the Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA), WHO Bangladesh, and JBRSOFT Limited.

The final stakeholder meeting represented an important project milestone, bringing stakeholders together around the development of a national platform for antimicrobial-use surveillance.

The platform is intended to support structured data collection, validation, and reporting in line with One Health surveillance needs.
